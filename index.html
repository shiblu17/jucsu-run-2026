About the Event

JUCSU RUN 2026: The Farewell

JU Campus Loop • 2026

The Last Lap at the Greenest Campus

Organized by the **Jahangirnagar University Central Students' Union (JUCSU)**, the **JUCSU RUN 2026: The Farewell** marks a historic chapter. This year, we are hosting our signature marathon as a tribute to the community, the memories, and the glorious natural canvas of Jahangirnagar University.

Whether you are a seasoned runner tackling our 10K Speed Run, or a hobbyist runner joining the 5K Run, you will sprint along beautiful lakes covered in migratory birds, beneath towering forest canopies, and over the signature red clay of the JU campus.

The Experience

JU Campus Circuit Highlights

The route consists of a loop around the green borders of the campus. 10K runners complete the full circuit, while 5K runners take a scenic shortcut halfway through the loop.

01
Shaheed Minar Square: The grand flag-off point. Features baggage counters, physical warm-up zone, and energy bars distribution.
02
Lakeside Corridor: Run alongside the university's famous lakes. Feel the morning breeze and watch the migratory birds.
03
Forest Canopy Path: Run under shades of towering trees near the Botanical Garden, providing pure oxygen and naturally cool temperatures.
04
Medical & Hydration Stations: Standard hydration setups located exactly at every 3 km containing mineral water, saline, bananas, and first-aid kits.

Race Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

📋 General Rules

  • Eligibility: Open to all students and adventure enthusiasts who are registered.
  • Conduct: Participants must follow the organizer's instructions and maintain decorum throughout the event.
  • Safety & Liability: Participants are responsible for their own well-being. Organizers are not liable for injuries, accidents, or loss of belongings.
  • Photography: By participating, you consent to being photographed/filmed for promotional and media coverage purposes.
  • Refunds: Registration fees are non-refundable except in the event of cancellation.

🏃 Race-Specific Rules

  • Age Limit: Must be at least 15 years old on race day (October 2, 2026).
  • Health Profile: Participants must be physically fit. A medical certificate may be requested.
  • Time Limits (Cut-off):
    • 10K Mini Marathon: 120 Minutes
    • 5K Run: 90 Minutes
  • Compliance: You must follow the designated route and checkpoints. Deviating will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Prohibited Items: Bicycles, skates, mobility aids (unless pre-approved), drugs, and smoking are strictly prohibited.
  • Reporting Time: Runners must report at the starting line at least 30 minutes before the start time.
